The idea of "Every Day a Little More" is to support you and help you become more fulfilled in all aspects of life. The Journal includes elements of projects inspired by behaviour therapy, art therapy, working with the inner child, meditation and other psychological tools for change. The prompts are designed in a way that allows you to explore on your own and to achieve insight and gain trust in your own Understanding.
